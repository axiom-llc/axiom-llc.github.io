System relationship

ASON evaluates and submits accepted plans to APEX. APEX validates and executes the submitted sequence while durably binding the ASON authorization identity and exact approved-plan digest before dispatch. RAG is accessed through explicit adapters and remains separate from policy enforcement and execution. Run the deterministic core-stack reference ↗
