Before kickoff

Clear expectations.
Direct answers.

Which package should we choose?

Choose Triage to isolate one failure, Reliability to test and patch one workflow, Hardening for up to two existing workflows, or custom-scoped Assurance for multi-system control work. RAG and integration issues remain eligible technical capabilities when included in an agreed scope.

What access and inputs are needed?

Provide the scoped repository or service, a working test environment, representative sanitized inputs, and a technical owner. Keep credentials in your approved secret store. Confirm data handling and access requirements before sharing sensitive material.

Can we use our existing LLM and infrastructure?

Yes. Model and deployment choices are yours. We verify the selected provider's interfaces and capabilities against the scope and acceptance criteria before commitment.

What does the fixed fee cover?

The listed deliverables and limits. Triage is diagnostic only; Reliability and Hardening include bounded changes and tests. Assurance is quoted separately. Additional scope, production deployment, usage charges, and ongoing support require a separate agreement.
